Driving Cycle Analysis

Urban driving cycle · Tehran
PythonData AnalysisVehicle DynamicsUrban Planning

Overview

The goal was to extract the driving cycle of Tehran from field data, and use it to find the optimal location for an electric bus charging station.

Approach

My team and I collected driving data and represented the Tehran driving cycle under three conditions: highway, urban, and congested. We split each trip into micro-trips, starting and ending whenever the speed dropped to zero and the vehicle stopped.
