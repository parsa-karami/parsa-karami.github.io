Indoor Tracking with BLE

~75 cm accuracy · GPS-denied
PythonnRFSignal ProcessingIoT

Overview

An indoor localization system that estimates the position of a target in environments where GPS is unavailable, using Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) beacons and received signal strength (RSSI).
